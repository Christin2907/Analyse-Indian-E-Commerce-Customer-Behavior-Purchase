🇮🇳 UrbanBazaar – E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive end-to-end Business Intelligence dashboard built in Power BI for a fictional Indian e-commerce company called UrbanBazaar.
The project focuses on customer journey analytics, conversion behavior, marketing performance, and predictive business insights.

📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard consists of multiple interactive report pages designed to support strategic business decisions through data-driven insights.

This dashboard was designed to:

Analyze customer purchasing behavior
Identify conversion bottlenecks
Evaluate marketing channel efficiency
Understand customer engagement patterns
Improve retention strategies
Support predictive business planning

📌 Key Insights
Discounts increase purchase probability
Returning users convert more frequently
Session duration impacts conversion likelihood
Marketing channels differ significantly in performance
Cart abandonment remains a major optimization opportunity
